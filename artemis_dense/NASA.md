## Public facts
- NASA (the National Aeronautics and Space Administration) is the US civil space agency, created
  in 1958.
- **The Artemis program** is NASA's program to return astronauts to the Moon.
  - Artemis I: uncrewed test flight of the SLS rocket and Orion spacecraft, launched November 16, 2022.
  - Artemis II: first crewed flight, launched April 1, 2026 from Kennedy Space Center with NASA
    astronauts Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover and Christina Koch and CSA astronaut Jeremy Hansen. It
    flew around the Moon and splashed down in the Pacific off San Diego on April 10, 2026
    (source: nasa.gov news release, "NASA Welcomes Record-Setting Artemis II Moonfarers Back to Earth").
  - Later missions aim to land crews on the Moon. Check nasa.gov for current dates, which change.
- NASA has tested laser communication in space, for example LCRD (launched December 2021) and
  DSOC on the Psyche spacecraft (launched October 2023). See [Laser.md](Laser.md).
