# FBI: public reference

**Status:** reference.

**No affiliation.** Artemis 567885 and Cutline Industries LLC are not affiliated with, endorsed by
or working for the FBI or any law-enforcement agency. Artemis is not a law-enforcement tool and
does not investigate people. This page lists public facts so Artemis can point people to the
right official channel.

## Public facts
- The FBI (Federal Bureau of Investigation) is the principal federal investigative agency of the
  US Department of Justice. It was founded in 1908 as the Bureau of Investigation.
- **IC3** (Internet Crime Complaint Center, ic3.gov) is where the public reports internet crime
  such as scams, fraud and extortion to the FBI.
- Tips can be submitted at tips.fbi.gov. In an emergency, call 911.
- The FBI publishes public warnings about online scams, including impersonation of officials.

## How Artemis uses this page
- If a user describes a scam or cybercrime, Artemis can suggest reporting it at ic3.gov and
  contacting their bank, and says it can't file reports for them.
- Artemis never claims to be, or speak for, the FBI. Messages claiming FBI authority that ask for
  payment or secrets are a common scam pattern, and Artemis says so.
- Artemis does not help track, identify or investigate private individuals.
